— What it is

A regenerative project

Espacio Timbó is a regenerative project located on the coast of Colonia, Uruguay, combining accommodation, education, art, permaculture, and ecological design. It was born with the purpose of contributing to the ecosocial regeneration of the territory we inhabit, promoting ways of life in harmony with nature.

Our space is designed under permaculture and regeneration principles, incorporating bioconstruction, an organic garden, ecological sanitation systems, responsible waste management, and gardens that promote biodiversity.

Each element of the place aims to demonstrate that it is possible to live consciously, reducing impacts and generating benefits for the land and the community.

"We place life at the center to regenerate the territory we inhabit and the bond we share with it."

— Regenerative Glamping

Living in
nature

Between the forest, the river, and Santa Ana beach, we offer accommodation experiences in yurts built by hand with natural and recyclable materials.

We have two yurts equipped with a kitchen and private bathroom, heating, ventilation, and outdoor spaces to rest, contemplate nature, and share around the fire.

The experience includes walking through the forest and the Santa Ana river, enjoying the beach, visiting the agroecological garden, and participating in regenerative practice workshops.

— About us

Julia &
Martín

Espacio Timbó was born in 2019 as a family-life project driven by the desire to find more conscious and regenerative ways of inhabiting the world. Since then, we have dedicated our time and energy to designing, building, and caring for this territory.

We believe in a way of life based on simplicity, self-sufficiency, the recovery of traditional knowledge and crafts, and continuous learning alongside nature.

"A life project born from the desire to live in coherence with our values."

Julia Mendoza

Anthropologist · Permaculturist · Photographer

Anthropologist and political scientist graduated from Universidad de los Andes (Colombia), with a Master’s in Environment from Universidad Complutense de Madrid. She worked in socio-environmental research with Instituto Humboldt, FAO, and the World Food Programme. Since 2019, she has driven Espacio Timbó through permaculture, bioconstruction, and agroforestry design.

Martín Goldszein

Permaculturist · Carpenter · Designer

Permaculturist, carpenter, blacksmith, and main builder of the infrastructure at Espacio Timbó. Trained in graphic design and web programming, with experience in electricity, sanitation, and sustainable solutions. He has participated in volunteering and learning activities linked to permaculture across Latin America.
